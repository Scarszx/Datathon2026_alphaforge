Financial planning

The title "financial planner" or its abbreviation "F.Pl." may be used by a person holding a diploma issued by the Institute of Financial Planning (the “Institute”) who has obtained a certificate from the AMF authorizing them to act in financial planning.

How to obtain a representative's certificate:
Step 1: Meet the minimum qualifications
Step 2: Apply for a certificate
